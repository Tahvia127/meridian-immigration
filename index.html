What we do

Five areas, done properly

We are a small firm and we say no to work outside these areas, because doing one thing well is worth more to you than doing everything adequately.

Family petitions

Spouse, parent, child and sibling petitions, adjustment of status, and consular processing.

Work visas

H-1B, L-1, O-1 and TN, plus employer sponsorship and labor certification.

Asylum

Affirmative and defensive asylum, withholding of removal, and Convention Against Torture claims.

Citizenship

Naturalization, the civics interview, and certificates of citizenship for children.

Removal defense

Immigration court representation, bond hearings, cancellation of removal, and appeals.

How it works
